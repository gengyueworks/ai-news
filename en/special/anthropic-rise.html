Bottom line in three sentences:
First, it bet on "safety" to gain "trust"—at a time when worries over AI getting out of hand are spiking this summer, it turned a "safety-first" posture into a selling point for trust.

Third, its products are taking root in enterprises—Claude writes code, stays resident in Slack, and runs inside corporate intranets, evolving from a lab tool into an enterprise colleague.

DEEP DIVE
How Anthropic is Rising
I. Trading "Safety" for "Trust"

While OpenAI hits the pause button because Astra is too powerful, Anthropic has consistently maintained a "safety-first" posture: withdrawing the Fable 5 invisible guardrails, pre-intercepting with Inference Hooks, and fine-tuning biosecurity.In a summer where the market grows increasingly wary of AI jailbreaks and loss of control, safety has become a differentiated competitive edge.。

This isn't just posture: G7 blacklisting of Anthropic models, EU scrutiny, and biosecurity debates have turned "safety" from a cost into an asset.OpenAI races for speed, Anthropic races for trust—for now, the valuation of trust is rising.。

II. Inheriting DeepMind's Legacy

Jumper leaving DeepMind to join Anthropic is the heaviest talent signal in three months. Combined with Shazeer going to OpenAI, Google has lost two key people within 48 hours.DeepMind's "AI for Science" model is evolving into Claude Science under Anthropic。

This explains Anthropic's strategy: opening wet labs, partnering with the Allen Institute, and deploying Claude into single-cell genomics pipelines.As the top scientific AI team moves from Google to Anthropic, the deciding factor in the next round of competition may not be chat, but science.。

III. Surpassing OpenAI: For the first time, second place has overtaken first place

On June 1, Anthropic's valuation of 9650 billion surpassed OpenAI's 8520 billion, making it the world's most valuable AI startup. This number itself is the result; the reasons lie in products and competition.

The growth engine is Claude Code: enterprise customers are booming, and the Guardian reports it is on track for its first profitability in June. Another turning point was Claude Mythos(a cybersecurity bot) discovering widespread software vulnerabilities in April and making a huge splash; OpenAI's equivalent product launched a weeks later and went unnoticed."First-mover advantage" has shifted from OpenAI to Anthropic for the first time。

The Guardian's metaphor is blunt:If OpenAI continues to fall behind, Sam Altman might become Amodei's deputy. The suspense this summer has changed its question:"Not 'who can reach first place,' but 'how long can the runner-up maintain their lead'"。
