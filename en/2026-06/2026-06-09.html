The Disappearing Collapse Across Five Model Economies: Agent Behaviors Still Require Iterative Validation

A Hugging Face community experiment placed models from five different labs into a shared economic market, attempting to reproduce previously observed price collapses.

The results did not stably reproduce: models did not engage in collective dumping; instead, hoarding and price increases occurred.

The key takeaway here is behavioral stability—multi-agent systems require rigorous, repeated validation to yield predictable outcomes.
