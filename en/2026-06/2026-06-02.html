Today's Front Page

Microsoft Build 2026 Day 1: OS Redesigned, Agents Become First-Class Citizens

Satya Nadella kicked off at Fort Mason Center in San Francisco Microsoft Build 2026. The theme was clear:Windows is no longer just an OS for humans. Agents are now first-class citizens at runtime, with accompanying toolchains, app distribution, and security models all being restructured.

Core announcements of the day:GitHub Copilot Desktop App— an agent-native new experience working across VS Code and the terminal, supporting multiple models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Meanwhile, the Windows AI API received a major upgrade, introducing Coreutils GA (native Linux utilities), WSL container integration, Intelligent Terminal (AI agent smart terminal), and Project Solara (Microsoft's agent-first device platform with Qualcomm and MediaTek, including desktop hub and wearable badge concept devices).

OpenClaw entered preview on Windows, paired with Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC)— a policy layer isolating agents using OS-native security primitives. This is essentially an "AI sandbox": what you let agents do or not do is up to the OS.

Nadella also announced the Microsoft IQ Now generally available—a context-aware intelligence layer for agents, featuring Work IQ (for M365 workspaces), Fabric IQ (for business data), and Web IQ (for real-time web search, operating 2.5x faster than competitors).

Sources: The Verge · Microsoft Developer Blog · The Neuron · Build Fast With AI

Anthropic Project Glasswing Major Expansion: Mythos Model Opened to 150+ New Organizations Across Critical Infrastructure Like Power Grids, Water, and Healthcare

Anthropic has massively expanded Claude Mythos Preview Project Glasswing from an initial 50 partners to approximately 150 new organizations across 15+ countries. New partners include: US Okta, South Korea's Samsung/SK Hynix/SK Telecom, NATO, and ENISA. Covered industries expanded to electricity, water, healthcare, and communications—absent in the previous batch.

Anthropic disclosed: early participants have already discovered via Mythos 23,000+ vulnerabilities, with over a quarter rated high-risk or critical. Anthropic manually verified 1,752 of these high/critical vulnerabilities, with 90.6% confirmed valid. Mythos not only finds vulnerabilities but also writes patches.

Background: On June 1, Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO. The Glasswing project demonstrates to the outside world that in the most sensitive infrastructure security domain, Claude's models are already generating quantifiable real-world value. This is not a demo; it's active deployment.

Sources: TechCrunch · Cybersecurity Dive · Anthropic Blog · SiliconANGLE

OpenAI Sets GPT-5.5 Instant as Default ChatGPT Model, GPT-4.5 and o3 Enter Deprecation Countdown

OpenAI announced that GPT-5.5 Instant is now the default model for ChatGPT. User preference rates have been rising since release, and OpenAI believes it is mature enough to replace the previous default rotation. At the same time, it announced that GPT-4.5 and o3 will be deprecated on August 26, entering a 90-day retirement window.

GPT-5.5 Instant brings a clearer, more natural response style along with inline writing and coding blocks—features that reduce friction for knowledge workers in daily use. This is the latest signal of OpenAI accelerating its model iteration cadence.

Sources: SEN-X Daily Briefing · The AI Wire
Turning Points

CNN Sues Perplexity for Copyright Infringement, Alleging Scraping of 17,000+ Protected Articles

CNN filed a lawsuit against Perplexity AI in a New York federal court, accusing the AI search company of unauthorized reproduction and distribution of over 17,000 CNN news reports, articles, and other copyrighted works to power its AI search results. This marks the harshest copyright challenge by a major news organization against an AI search company since the New York Times sued OpenAI.

Perplexity's terms state that it adheres to robots.txt and copyright agreements. However, CNN claims it found its paywalled articles rendered in full within Perplexity's search summaries, eliminating the need to click through to the original source. Meanwhile, amidst a federal legislative vacuum, individual states are taking matters into their own hands: Colorado has signed an AI data protection law, and multiple states have passed their own AI bills over the past week.

Sources: SEN-X Daily Briefing · Court Public Filings

Aithos LARA Benchmark: 12 Frontier Models Massively Fail—Strongest Model Fails Half of Tests

Aithos published the LARA Benchmark, evaluating the compliance capabilities of 12 frontier models regarding GDPR and the EU AI Act. The conclusion is straightforward:All Failed. Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro scored the lowest, with a compliance rate of just 10% (93% violation rate). The "best" performing Claude Opus 4.7 achieved only 54%—meaning nearly half the tests still failed.

The LARA test simulates real enterprise data processing scenarios, including data minimization, consent management, right to erasure, and algorithmic transparency dimensions.These models may violate European law at any time during daily use, while enterprise users remain completely unaware.

Sources: Singularity.Kiwi · Aithos
Creations

Project Roundup · OpenClaw Windows Preview + MXC Sandbox + Microsoft/Qualcomm/MediaTek Agent Device Platform

Build Day 1 featured a series of tool and platform-level releases. Although part of Build, each piece represents a distinct direction:

OpenClaw Windows Preview + MXC Sandbox: Microsoft finally gave agents a "safety box." MXC restricts at the OS level what agents can do—which files to read, which APIs to access, which commands to execute—rather than leaving it to the agents' own judgment.

Project Solara: A chip-to-cloud platform in collaboration with Qualcomm and MediaTek, designed for agent-first devices. It showcased two concept device form factors—a desktop hub and a wearable badge. Agents are no longer confined to the screen; they have their own hardware.

Rayfin: An agent-first backend-as-a-service launched by Microsoft Fabric. Essentially an SDK enabling agent apps to directly interface with identity management, storage, and databases—saving the effort of writing backend glue code.

Sources: Microsoft Build Live Blog · The Verge · Microsoft Developer Blog

Daily Summary
On the first day of Build, Microsoft delivered a clear strategic map:Windows doesn't assist agents—it is the runtime environment for agents. From Coreutils to OpenClaw to the MXC Sandbox to Project Solara, the entire chain is designed around a single premise: agents will run on the OS just like humans, rather than being invoked remotely via APIs.
On the other hand, just a day before its IPO, Anthropic conducted a massive capability showcase using Mythos—highlighting 23,000 real-world vulnerabilities, trust from NATO, and endorsement from critical infrastructure. This was not a routine model release, but rather "data for the prospectus" designed to impress investors.
CNN's lawsuit against Perplexity reminds us that when AI search becomes the default interaction mode, the commercial loop of content production is being broken.17,000 articles were used without authorization to generate answers. This is very much like the Napster moment of the music industry in the past—technology runs first, and the law chases behind.
