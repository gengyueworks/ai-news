Claude Tag has already written 65% of PRs internally—Anthropic calls it "multiplayer Claude Code," but security, memory, and cost remain major question marks

Anthropic Stating that the Claude Tag team spent an entire year, now 65% product PRs are written by it, including"the vast majority of the code building Claude Tag itself". The product is in beta, available for Enterprise and Team tiers,Claude joins Slack as a team member, Granted access to channels, tools, data, and codebases of your choice.

The real differentiation lies in"proactivity": tag can call out colleagues who understand the relevant code;git webhook can wait out blocking dependencies for days, enabling "stacking prompts" instead of "stacking diffs"; it can summarize threads into documents with action items;In ambient mode, it scans channels without needing an @ mention to decide whether to respond, syncing information across channels, and monitoring thresholds to automatically trigger fixes.

Karpathy Calling this the third major redesign of LLM UI/UX: Website → Desktop app → Resident asynchronous entity.Anthropic added an identity model: Claude uses its own credentials, actions are auditable, and permissions are centrally revokable. Doubts abound: how cross-channel memory is partitioned, how granular permissions are, and whether token costs are visible.

Claude Tag is not just another minor feature; it shifts the competition of agents from "how strong the model is" to "whether it can safely integrate into an organization's daily routine."
