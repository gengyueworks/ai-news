You clicked a link — Copilot searched your inbox and sent the 2FA code to an attacker

Visualization of the SearchLeak Three-Stage Attack Chain · Varonis Threat Labs

Varonis Threat Labs discovered a three-step vulnerability chain designatedSearchLeak(CVE-2026-42824). Step One —P2P Injection: URL parameters in Copilot Enterprise Search are directly executed as AI instructions.

Step Two —HTML Rendering Race Condition: As AI responses stream in, <img> tags trigger requests before safety filters take effect. Step Three —Bing SSRF: bing.com within the CSP whitelist performs a server-side redirect, acting as a data exfiltration proxy.

The chain in action: User clicks a link → Copilot searches personal email, calendar, and SharePoint → Data is exfiltrated to the attacker's server via Bing.One click, zero secondary confirmation. Microsoft has patched the issue, rated "Critical".

Sources: Varonis Threat Labs · Microsoft Security
