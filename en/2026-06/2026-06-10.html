Today's Summary

Today's briefing captures several key signals.

First trend: Creative tools are gaining a sense of space.

Mythos combines mapping, writing, and resource organization, demonstrating that AI creative tools are evolving from text boxes into structured workstations.

Second trend: Agents are becoming executable systems.

Scheduled tasks, environment variables, model routing, and custom CLI agents are all transitioning AI from one-off chats to stable workflows.

Third trend: Cost and observability are moving to the core of products.

AgentsView's pricing settings, OpenRouter Advisor, and IDE integrations indicate that teams will increasingly care about the value of every model call.

Tomorrow we will continue monitoring two areas: how creator tools organize complex projects, and how agent platforms solidify execution, permissions, and cost controls.
