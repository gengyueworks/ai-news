WAIC 2026 30-Day Countdown: Over 300 AI Products to Make Global Debut in Shanghai This July

On June 17, the 30-day countdown press conference for the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) was held in Shanghai. Scheduled from July 17 to 20 across the Expo, Zhangjiang, and Xuhui riverside zones, the event is co-hosted by the Shanghai Municipal Government alongside 10 national ministries, including the Ministry of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

The conference will feature over 140 thematic forums, gathering 1,400+ international guests and 1,100+ global enterprises. It will showcase 3,000+ exhibits, including over 300 global debuts of AI products. The conference also released its first AI open ecosystem platform app, "Hi WAIC," opening registration and ticketing.

  • Academic Lineup: Turing Award winner Andrew Yao serves as Conference Chairman, and Father of Reinforcement Learning Richard Sutton serves as International Co-Chairman.

  • New Sections: Inaugural OPC exhibition area featuring 180 enterprises; five major track ecosystem matrices debut for the first time.

  • City Integration: WAIC City Walk connects all 16 districts of Shanghai, extending the AI experience from exhibition halls to city streets.

    GLM-5.2 Becomes the First Open-Source Model to Cross the 80% Terminal-Bench Threshold—Yet Closed-Source Leaders Still Lead by 4 Points

    The r/LocalLlama discussion board put GLM-5.2 's open-source weights on the Terminal-Bench 2.1 leaderboard:81.0 points, making it the first open-source model in the chart to cross the 80% dashed line, outperforming all other open-source contenders and even surpassing Gemini 3.1 Pro.Closed-source models still lead: Claude Opus 4.8 scored 85.0, and GPT-5.5 scored 84.0— narrowing the gap from "whether they can compete" to "just a few points behind" for the first time.

    The community reminds users to check versions first: Terminal-Bench 2.1 is a"lenient version"with relaxed timeout and problem rules, making cross-version scores artificially inflated. Conclusive judgments must wait for Terminal-Bench 3. Another debate centers on whether "downloadable" equals "runnable locally": the weights carry an MIT license, but 753B total parameters in the MoE require 176~890GB of memory, making it unrealistic for 99% of users.

    While the score itself isn't explosive, it marks the first time open-source weights stand on the same coding benchmark chart as closed-source models—serving as the starting point for all subsequent GLM-5.2 breakthroughs this week.

    Gemini CLI Shuts Down Today: 100K-Star Open-Source Project Shifts to Closed-Source Alternative

    Google announced that Gemini CLI will discontinue service on June 18, affecting free users as well as Pro and Ultra subscribers. Its replacement, Antigravity CLI, is a closed-source rewrite in Go and lacks complete functional parity at launch. Enterprise customers retain dual-access rights. During its run, Gemini CLI accumulated over 6,000 community-merged PRs and over 100,000 GitHub stars.

  • Affected: Free, Pro, and Ultra subscribers, alongside Gemini Code Assist subscribers

  • Alternative: Antigravity CLI (Closed-source Go rewrite, temporarily incomplete functionality, original version restricted to enterprise customers)

  • Community Cost: A code repository with 6,000+ merged PRs and 100,000+ stars; community contributors are excluded from the new product.

    Google Adds Streaming Voice to Gemini: AI Can Speak While Thinking Without Waiting for Full Sentences

    On June 17, Google added streaming voice generation capabilities to the gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview model. Developers can invoke it via the streamGenerateContent method, allowing the AI to stream audio data as it generates without waiting for the entire audio clip to assemble. In voice assistant, real-time translation, and intelligent customer service scenarios, the AI responds immediately after the user finishes speaking.

  • Invocation Method: streamGenerateContent or the stream:true parameter in the Interactions API

  • Applicable Scenarios: Voice assistants, real-time translation, intelligent customer service—any product requiring instant voice feedback

  • Latency Improvement: 1–2 second wait for full audio generation → 200–300 milliseconds for first streaming audio output

    OpenAI Revenue Multiplies 3.5x Yet Remains Unprofitable: Half of R&D Expenses Paid to Microsoft

    Ars Technica reports that SEC filings prepared by OpenAI for its upcoming IPO have leaked. In 2025, revenue reached $13.07 billion (up from $3.7 billion in 2024), with an operating loss of $20.92 billion. R&D expenditure stood at $19.18 billion, of which $10.59 billion was paid to Microsoft for computing power and licensing. The Sora video generation model was shut down in March 2026, just 15 months after its launch.

  • Growth Comparison: 3.5x revenue growth vs. 1.4x wider losses—revenue growth cannot outpace cash burn

  • Largest Single Expense: Half of R&D budget paid to Microsoft (compute + licensing)

  • Sora Shut Down: Video model discontinued 15 months after launch

    WeChat AI Agent: I say 'Cantonese restaurant for 3 people tomorrow noon,' and it finds and books a restaurant for me

    According to The Information, Tencent is testing an AI Agent within WeChat. When a user says, 'Cantonese restaurant for 3 people tomorrow noon with a budget of 500 yuan,' the Agent automatically searches for restaurants, compares prices, places orders, and sends confirmations across multiple mini-programs. The project entered grayscale testing in June, covering millions of mini-programs in the WeChat ecosystem. This will be the first case of deep AI Agent integration in a Chinese social platform.

  • Usage: User speaks a single sentence; Agent searches restaurants, compares prices, places orders, and sends confirmation across mini-programs

  • Coverage: Millions of mini-programs in the WeChat ecosystem can be invoked

  • Timeline: Grayscale testing starting in June
