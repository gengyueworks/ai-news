Meta acquires nearly half of Scale AI for $14.3 billion; 28-year-old founder Alexandr Wang to lead superintelligence division

Meta acquires 49% of Scale AI for $14.3 billion, with valuation jumping from $13.8 billion to $29 billion.

  • Wang dropped out of MIT at 19 to start a company, and will now lead Meta's newly established superintelligence team

  • Google plans to cut ties with Scale AI due to conflicts of interest

    Creations

    VoxCPM: Multilingual speech generation using a Tokenizer-Free architecture

    VoxCPM 2 utilizes a Tokenizer-Free architecture to bypass the discretization steps of traditional TTS models—processing raw audio tokens directly to avoid information loss introduced by tokenizers. Weights have been open-sourced across three scenarios: multilingual TTS, voice cloning, and creative sound design.

  • Tokenizer-Free architecture, bypassing discretization

  • Supports multilingual TTS / voice cloning / creative sound design

  • Open-source weights, locally deployable

    zvec: Alibaba open-sources in-process vector database with zero external dependencies

    zvec implements in-process vector indexing using C++, eliminating network I/O and achieving latency an order of magnitude lower than standalone vector databases. It is ideal for lightweight scenarios in RAG applications that do not require distributed deployment—embed it directly into applications without managing vector database clusters.

  • Runs in-process in C++, zero network I/O

  • Suitable for lightweight RAG / semantic search, no cluster maintenance required

  • Open-source and embeddable

    Federal court orders OpenAI to preserve all ChatGPT logs, including user-deleted conversations

    New York federal judge Ona Wang orders OpenAI to indefinitely retain all ChatGPT output logs—including user-deleted conversations, "temporary chats," and API call records. Prompted by news organizations like the NYT concerned that infringing users might delete conversational traces. OpenAI calls the order "unprecedented," forcing the company to violate its privacy commitments and exposing sensitive data of hundreds of millions of users to breach risks.

  • Judge Wang issues preservation order on May 13, covering all logs for ChatGPT Free, PLUS, Pro, and API

  • OpenAI states the order forces it to violate privacy agreements with users and global privacy regulations

  • Users panic on LinkedIn and X; cybersecurity experts call it an "unacceptable security risk"
