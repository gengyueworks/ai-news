Research Release · 6/30 · Official Release · Meta AI

Meta Brain-Computer Interface Brain2Qwerty v2: Non-invasive real-time decoding of full sentences, 78% word accuracy in best subject—still lab scanning, not wearable yet

Meta AI Released June 30, Brain2Qwerty v2: A system for real-time sentence decoding from raw brain signals, decoding "words and semantics" rather than mere characters. Data: Overall word accuracy approx. 61%, best performing subject 78%, training data from 9 volunteers in a controlled typing scenario.

The heaviest engineering feat isn't consumer usability, but bridging raw neural signal modeling with language modeling,making sentence-level decoding viable in the lab. Meta will open-source the training code for v1/v2, and BCBL hosts the v1 dataset. Another easily overlooked detail:A coding agent workflow called Auto Researchdiscovered an improvement that pushed word error rates below standard HPO on its own.

Non-invasive real-time mind reading is still far off, but "mind reading + intent reading" has reproducible open-source code for the first time—lowering the barrier from a few lab teams to the broader research community.

Open-source models get "subscriptionized": Cline offers GLM-5.2 family bundle for $9.99/mo, Cognition cuts coding costs by 35% via model mixing

Two news items on June 30 piece together a trend: the entry point for open-source models is shifting from "managing individual API keys" to "buying a monthly pass for unlimited use."Cline Launches $9.99/mo pass, bundled with discounted access to GLM-5.2, DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen, etc.
