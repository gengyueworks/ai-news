Mistral releases OCR 4: 170 languages, inline confidence—yet the "SOTA" claim is immediately debunked, ranking only third

Mistral Released OCR 4 on June 24: featuring structured extraction, bounding boxes, block classification, and inline confidence, supporting 170 languages. Baidu's Unlimited-OCR launched concurrently, adding two heavyweight contenders to the OCR model race in a single day.

However, Niels Rogge immediately challenged the "strongest" claim: on the public OlmOCRBench leaderboard, it currently ranks only third, behind open-source solutions like Chandra OCR 2.

Document parsing is the most unassuming yet fiercely contested area in open source—whoever masters "enabling agents to understand PDFs" controls the foundation of all enterprise agents.
