Axios / TechCrunch / Reuters · June 26

OpenAI Releases Three New GPT-5.6 Models, U.S. Government Approves Releases Customer by Customer

On June 26, OpenAI launched its next-generation model Sol, Terra, Luna, announcing on the same day that all three will only be available to approximately 20 companies, with every new customer onboarding requiring U.S. government sign-off. According to OpenAI, this is a short-term measure requested by the U.S. government.

Bloomberg · June 26

AI Makes Silicon Valley Efficient, Anxious, and Unable to Log Off—"An Agent Watched Over My Other Agents While I Slept"

Serial entrepreneur Matt Van Horn, father of four, never shuts his laptop. He runs half a dozen AI agents simultaneously, every 10 minute asking him what to do next. Left running during kids' soccer practice, school drop-offs, and vacations.While he slept, one agent watched over his other agents.

What drives him isn't efficiency, it's FOMO—AI improves every day, and if I'm not present, I might get left behind. Efficiency gains translate into longer work hours, not shorter ones.

  • Van Horn runs 6 agents simultaneously using Anthropic's Claude Code
  • FOMO becomes the new driver for overtime: AI is progressing → I cannot stop
  • Researchers find: Time saved by AI is swallowed by AI anxiety
Sources: Bloomberg · bloomberg.com
Bloomberg · June 25

When AI Falls Short, Ford Rehires Retired "Graybeard" Quality Inspectors

On June 25, Bloomberg reported that Ford has been rehiring approximately 350 senior quality inspectors—because AI failed to handle the job. Internally referred to as "gray beard" veteran masters, they rely on intuition and experience to catch defects missed by AI. As one executive bluntly stated,some judgments currently still rely on humans. Against the backdrop of Ford recently securing a J.D. Power 16 first-place ranking in years, the quality bar remains tightly strung.

Sources: Bloomberg · bloomberg.com

Figma Unveils Motion, Shaders, and Code Layers at Config 2026 Motion, Shaders and Code Layers

At Config 2026 on June 24, Figma introduced the animation tool Motion, shaders Shaders and Code Layers. Motion integrates motion effects directly into design files, while Code Layers further blurs the line between "design" and "code."

For designers, this means throwing fewer"please implement this" over the fence; for developers, it means receiving mockups that are closer to runnable code.
