Google DeepMind · Talent Earthquake

Google DeepMind's golden generation walks out: the people best at building models collectively turn to "automated discovery"

Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le left Google DeepMind to co-found Discovery Loop, a public-benefit company with a mission of automating machine learning, science and engineering; Demis Hassabis moves from CEO to DeepMind chairman and Alphabet chief scientist.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Discovery Loop's seed round was led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures, with Lightspeed, Kleiner Perkins, Doerr Capital and Alphabet participating. Jeff Dean's announcement tweet took 3.2M views and 15K likes that day. Koray Kavukcuoglu was promoted to SVP, taking over Gemini and frontier research. From writing code to writing a manifesto, Google could not keep them.

AINews asked two blunt questions: why could this not be done inside Google? DeepMind has over 1,000 co-authored Gemini papers alone, yet these four left with a manifesto. Add six months without a Gemini Pro update, John Jumper leaving for Anthropic, Noam Shazeer for OpenAI, David Silver and Denny Zhou departing in succession. Andrew Ng and Nathan Lambert both called it a historic turning point for Google AI: automated scientific research moves from side quest to main quest for the first time.

When the people best at building models collectively turn to "automated discovery", AGI's next competition may not be about parameter counts at all.

Google DeepMind's golden generation walks out.

Suno · Music Rights

Suno adds watermarks to AI music: to go legit, first learn to leave traces

Suno announced new watermarking and fingerprinting technology alongside a new download policy to curb AI-generated spam music; CEO Mikey Shulman said it will connect to "industry standards now taking shape".

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

After last year's settlement with Warner Music, Suno had previewed tightening downloads: non-subscribers lose free exports, monthly downloads capped. Bringing watermarks and fingerprints together amounts to stamping every AI-generated song so rights holders can trace it, platforms can recognize it, and listeners can judge it.

When AI music wants to move from "gray zone" into legitimate channels, transparency and traceability become the entrance ticket.
