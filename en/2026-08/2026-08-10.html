08-10 · Unitree · A-Shares
Robot valuations have changed, and Unitree lists first: A-shares start pricing "model-fed robots"

Unitree opened its IPO subscription today at 150.80 yuan per share, implying a market cap of about 61B yuan, aiming to raise about 6.1B; the issue P/E ratio is 219x. In the strategic placement sit the social security fund and DeepSeek.

When robot valuations pivot from "building hardware" to "feeding models", A-shares' first humanoid stock is really pricing the whole track.

08-10 · Jobs · Signal
AI cuts entry-level jobs: a tragedy of the cognitive commons is underway

A new worry is circulating: when AI eats junior roles first, newcomers lose the ground to practice on, nobody learns fundamentals hands-on anymore, and the industry's accumulation of know-how starts to break.

It has been called a tragedy of the cognitive commons: everyone benefits from an environment where newcomers keep arriving, yet every company individually finds "deploy AI first" cheapest. The costs AI saves may be overdrawing the whole industry's future fundamentals.

08-10 · GitHub Models · Retired
GitHub Models is retired: a portrait of one era, a footnote to the next

Simon Willison noted that GitHub Models has completed its retirement: the model playground and the unified API across LLM providers are formally closed.

The service once let code in GitHub Actions call any provider's models with in-environment API keys. Its closure reads as a signal: how developers reach models has moved from "platforms build bridges" to "bring your own quota and connect directly".
