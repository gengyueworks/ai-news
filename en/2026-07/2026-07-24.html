07-24 · Hugging Face · Open-source Data

The Stack v3 Released, The largest open-source code dataset in history, 5 trillion tokens ready for direct training

Hugging FaceResearchers release The Stack v3:114 TB of raw data,2.24 million repositories,440 million files,770 languages, approx. after deduplication and filtering 5 trillion tokens.

Compared to v2, the filtered corpus grew from approx. 5500 billion tokens to 5 trillion, nearly a 10 fold increase; C++ saw the fastest growth (×15), TypeScript (×7.5), Rust (×7). Operational changes include direct content inline, removal of restrictively licensed code, and a dedicated"ready-to-train"shard.

Its significance lies in a "lower floor", not "larger scale":Previously, corpus data was the closed-source camp's biggest invisible barrier for training competitive code models; this dataset flattens the barrier instantly..Open data + open weights + open inference stack happening simultaneously in July are entirely dismantling the moat of code models.

Sources: AINews · Hugging Face / The Stack v3
07-24 · Etched · Funding

Etched raises 3 million USD, focusing solely on inference, valuation hits $10.3 billion

EtchedCompletes 3 million Series C, valuation at 103 billion, to expand production of its custom Transformer inference chips and open a 8 sq ft,10 MW new facility.Its slogan is direct: No training cards,run the world's inference”.

This funding bets on a contrarian route: while most buy general GPUs, Etched builds application-specific chips hardwired for attention computation. It bets that—when inference demand is driven up by agents and long context, the waste of general-purpose cards will be so massive that dedicated chips warrant their own production lines..

Combined with the momentum of SambaNova and Groq, the "non-Nvidia" inference route is securing real capital at scale.The training card landscape is set, while the inference card spot remains open—everyone is betting on "who can drive down per-inference costs to a level others cannot ignore".
