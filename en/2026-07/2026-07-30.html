07-30 · Policy / Smart Economy

Smart economy written into reports, but execution is the real test One person with Agents can do the work of a 100-person team, but the real uphill battle has just begun

Smart economy written into the report.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

The government work report first introduced the new form of "smart economy"; NDRC Director Zheng Shanjie stated at a press conference that by the end of the 14th Five-Year Plan, the scale of AI-related industries will grow to 10 over 1 trillion yuan. Zhejiang leveraged this to introduce the "One-Person Company (OPC)" model, targeting an AI core industry scale of 1.2 trillion by 2030.

In Zhejiang's pilot program, "One-Person Company" is not just rhetoric—Xihu and Binjiang districts in Hangzhou have carved out dedicated communities: computing vouchers issued monthly, streamlined business registration, and zero-deposit office spaces and shared labs.

An engineer who registered an OPC in Binjiang said that projects which previously required a 7-person team can now be handled alone with the help of two Agent assistants. "Expanding the team means losing control, but shrinking it gives you the courage to take on anything"—this marks the first time the "intelligent economy" has found a concrete physical office as its footnote.

When "one person + several Agents" is written into policy language, the startup threshold drops from "assembling a team" to "plugging in a few tools."

Sources: CCTV.com · Chao News

07-29 · CNIPA · Regulation

14th Five-Year Plan opens institutional pathways for AI patents and BCI applications, but the real hard battle has just begun

At the State Council Information Office press conference on July 29, CNIPA Deputy Commissioner Rui Wenbiao stated that patent examination standards will be refined for emerging industries such as "AI+" embodied AI and brain-computer interfaces, the patentability of AI-generated content will be studied, and the construction of AI patent pools will be guided. Currently, AI and big data-related goods and service items have reached 900 items.

A step forward in patent examination standards is equivalent to issuing a "protectable" ticket to brain-computer interfaces and embodied AI in the lab.

Data intellectual property registration, Zhejiang takes the lead with 1,000 cases

The "14th Five-Year Plan for Intellectual Property Protection and Utilization" was recently issued. Zhejiang took the national lead in releasing application guidelines for data IP registration in the AI field, having registered over 1000 high-quality datasets and implemented cases such as data asset capitalization and cross-border circulation.

07-30 · Hangzhou · One-Person Company

Hangzhou launches dedicated OPC communities, Single registration, AI agents as employees

Hangzhou establishes dedicated innovation communities for OPCs (One-Person Companies), offering computing vouchers to lower startup costs and implementing streamlined business approvals. A single individual can register a company, deploy agents, and handle R&D, marketing, and customer service. Tasks that previously required 10 people to run can now be handled solo.

Policies have turned "one person is a company" from a meme into a registrable reality—putting individuals on the same starting line as corporations for the first time.

Vision
07-30 · Indie Developer · Experience Change

Building products with agents, My company's entire workforce consists of a few agents

An indie developer delegated product research, frontend, copywriting, and customer service to different agents, building in days what used to take a small team weeks. "Now I am the whole company, and its entire staff," they noted.

Detailing the workflow: the research agent scrapes user feedback from 6 vertical sites every midnight into 3 actionable tasks; the frontend agent writes PRs for two items during his lunch break; the copywriting agent turns updates into 800-word WeChat articles; the customer service agent answers 11 user queries while he sleeps.

He noted the biggest surprise wasn't just speed, but that "my focus on product details expanded threefold—things I previously lacked time for are now pre-screened by agents."

When tools can attend meetings, write code, and reply to clients for you, the bottleneck of a one-person company is no longer manpower, but imagination.

Sources: Chao News
