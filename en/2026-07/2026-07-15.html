07-15 · Meituan · Open Source · Domestic Cluster

Meituan open-sources LongCat-2.0, with 1.6 trillion parameters running on a domestic 50,000-card cluster, but the real hard battle has just begun

Meituan's technical team released LongCat-2.0, with total parameters of 1.6 trillion, average activation of 48 billion, adopting LongCat sparse attention and N-gram Embedding, natively supporting 1 million token context.It was pre-trained from scratch, with training and inference completed entirely on a domestic 50,000-card cluster, accompanied by inference code optimized for domestic GPUs. This is the first open-source model at this scale to run the entire training and inference lifecycle on domestic computing power. For teams worried about computing power, this means an implementation path independent of overseas cards,pushing 'can we build and run it ourselves' a big step forward.

07-15 · Microsoft · Self-Developed Model · De-Dependency

Microsoft tucks self-developed MAI into Excel and Outlook, quietly replacing OpenAI and Anthropic

According to Bloomberg, Microsoft has begun replacing original OpenAI and Anthropic models with its self-developed MAI series in Excel and Outlook, with hundreds of thousands of AI requests handled by its own models every week.The direct reason is cost—Microsoft AI lead Mustafa Suleyman bluntly stated that the fees paid to Anthropic annually are too high, and the goal is to gradually eliminate this expense. This also means cracks are beginning to appear in the tight bond between Microsoft and OpenAI: the self-developed model has already integrated into GitHub Copilot, and the next step will be Teams voice transcription. For Office users, the model has changed but the experience may not,yet the underlying bill has been rewritten.

07-15 · CAC · On-Device Filing · Compliance

CAC adds 7 new on-device generative AI filings for smartphones, Apple Intelligence, Xiaoyi, and BlueLM included

On July 15, the CAC announced new filings for 7 on-device generative AI services, covering Apple Intelligence, Huawei Xiaoyi, vivo BlueLM, Xiaomi HyperOS, and Nubia Doubao.On-device AI compliance is accelerating—models run locally with data staying on-device, facilitating regulatory oversight. On the same day, the Interim Provisions on the Management of Artificial Intelligence Anthropomorphic Interactive Services took effect, systematically regulating persistent emotional interaction services for the first time. Doubao and Qwen previously took offline user-customized anthropomorphic agents. On-device AI plus official filing is becoming the default threshold for mobile AI.

07-15 · NVIDIA × Hugging Face · Robotics & Open-Source Toolchain

NVIDIA and Hugging Face integrate GR00T 1.7, Giving robots an open-source toolchain

NVIDIA and Hugging Face have integrated GR00T 1.7 (a vision-language-action model for humanoid robots) and Isaac Teleop (an open-source framework for collecting human demonstration data) into Hugging Face's LeRobot library.Developers can use the LeRobot workflow to post-train GR00T and redeploy it to real robots, while Isaac Teleop uses a unified pipeline to collect interoperable demonstration data. Combined, it forms a complete open-source pipeline spanning remote collection, simulation training, model fine-tuning, and real-world deployment. Connecting 3 million robotics developers and 16 million AI developers, lowering the barrier to entry for embodied AI research once againlowering the barrier to entry for embodied AI research once again.

07-15 · Mistral · Formal Verification · Open Weights

Mistral releases Leanstral 1.5, Achieving max score on miniF2F formal verification

Mistral releases Leanstral 1.5, a Mixture of Experts model built specifically for Lean 4 formal verification, featuring a total of 119 billionparameters with 6 billion active, released under the Apache 2.0 license. It achieved 100% on both the miniF2F validation and test sets, solved 587 out of 672 problems in PutnamBench, and set new records on FATE-H and FATE-X.Turning the ability to "prove mathematical propositions" into an open-source model benefits far more than just mathematics—hardcore scenarios like code correctness and protocol verification are in dire need of this verifiable reliability. Small active parameters also mean it can run on lower computational resources,Transforming theorem proving from a niche craft into a callable capability.

07-15 · Research · Long-Term Memory · Open Source

Post-00s team builds HMS Holographic Memory, Reaches SOTA on dual long-term memory leaderboards

Shadoweave, a team of post-00s scholars from institutions such as CMU, Tsinghua University, and Shanghai AI Lab, has released the HMS Holographic Memory system, engineering the human instinct of "recollection." It topped both the LongMemEval and LoCoMo long-term memory benchmarks simultaneously (accuracy 92.8% / 93.5%), proposed the vision of an "AI-era memory protocol," and open-sourced the SDK.Most AI "memory" remains limited to short-term dialogue windows, whereas HMS treats cross-day, cross-task long-term dynamic modeling as its core problem. For an agent to truly understand a user,it must first learn to remember them.

07-15 · Paramount / Disney · Streaming Strategy · Visual Giants

The Streaming Dilemma of Visual Giants, The Old Kings Demolished Their Own Moats

Paramount and Disney once made steady profits by "producing content and selling copyrights." The streaming wars rewrote all of this—they poured money into building their own platforms to rival Netflix, resulting in severe market overcapacity. Now a turning point has arrived: Paramount Skydance 1100 acquired Warner Bros. Discovery for billions of dollars, only to face a joint antitrust lawsuit from 12 states; meanwhile, Wells Fargo suggested that Disney should simply scrap Disney+ and focus solely on content licensing, claiming this could boost its market value by 40%.

For creators of visual content, this is a reminder: never bet your fate on a platform built by others.
