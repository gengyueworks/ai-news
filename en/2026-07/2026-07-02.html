Anthropic · June 30 – July 1

Four rivals sit down to establish a unified ruler for "jailbreaks"

A jailbreak is using tricks to induce a model to say or do things it shouldn't. In the past, companies argued over "how severe this jailbreak is" without any recognized standard.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Anthropic Co-founded by Amazon, Microsoft, Google and other partners, proposing a unified industry framework for scoring "jailbreak severity." The goal is to allow safety issues across different companies and models to be compared on the same ruler.

Several fiercest competitors have rarely wanted to unify their stance on safety evaluation.With a common ruler, whether models are truly safe becomes comparable, and you and I as users have a basis to rely on.
