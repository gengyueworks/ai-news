Front Page
07-20 · MIIT · Policy

"AI + Manufacturing" defined, but the real battlefield is the real economy The real economy is the primary battlefield for artificial intelligence, but the real tough battle is just beginning

On July 20, a national symposium of industry and information technology authorities clarified: manufacturing is the "primary battlefield" for AI applications. The meeting called for the in-depth implementation of the"AI + Manufacturing"action plan, introducing lightweight, low-cost, and easily deployable industry solutions to lower the adoption barrier for small and medium-sized enterprises; meanwhile, promoting the popularization of next-generation smart devices such as humanoid robots and brain-computer interfaces, and strengthening national-level AI open-source communities.In H1, the value added of China's above-scale equipment manufacturing increased by 9.3% year-on-year, and high-tech manufacturing by 13.3%, both significantly outpacing overall industrial growth.

Kling AI secures nearly $3B in Series A, largest single round for global video foundation model

On July 2, Kuaishou's Kling AI closed nearly 30 billion USD in Series A financing, with a post-money valuation of approximately 180 billion USD, setting a record for the largest financing round by a global video AI company.Current ARR approaches 5 billion USD, with global users exceeding 1 hundred million. This round was co-led by CPE, Tencent, and CITIC Securities, with Middle Eastern capital BlueFive participating for the first time.
