NVIDIA · July 2

NVIDIA's TwoTower: Generation speeds up by 2.42 x, maintaining quality 98.7 %

NVIDIA Open-source Nemotron-Labs-TwoTower: Turning a 30B model into a diffusion language model with dual-replica parallel token writing—a frozen context model plus a trained writing model—without retraining from scratch..

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Official figures show generation is 2.42 x faster, retaining 98.7% of the original model's quality. The underlying principle is simple: splitting context understanding and output generation into two roles (the former fixed, the latter specially trained), eliminating redundant forward passes during generation.

The implications for the application layer are direct:Speed and quality are no longer mutually exclusive.When architectural engineering achieves both speed and quality simultaneously, inference costs have further room to drop.Model competition is shifting from 'who can train it' to 'who can run it cheaper'..

Insights · New Approaches and Real Costs in This AI Wave
Thinking Machines Lab / Bridgewater · Original June 30 · Trending This Week

A fine-tuned small model beats all frontier LLMs in financial judgment—and is nearly 14 x cheaper

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab research in collaboration with Bridgewater Associates delivers a counterintuitive result: a specialized small model fine-tuned on high-quality expert-annotated data outperforms all tested frontier LLMs on real-world investment judgment tasks.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

After careful prompt tuning, frontier models (Gemini, Claude, and GPT variants) achieved an average accuracy of only 78%, falling short of the 80% trust threshold required by analysts. Meanwhile, the fine-tuned specialized small model reached 84.7%, with an error rate lower than the strongest frontier model by 29.8%, while costing only 1/13.8.

The intuition that "bigger is better and chasing the latest frontier is always right" is being challenged. For many specific tasks, a fine-tuned small model can be both more accurate and much cheaper.

Fine-tuned specialized small model: 84.7% vs. Top frontier model: 78.2%, at just 1/13.8 of the cost. Data: Thinking Machines Lab / Bridgewater AIA Labs.

MacTalk via WeChat · July 2

Kuaishou releases AgentX: Recommendation systems begin self-iteration

Kuaishou AgentX, targeting industrial recommendation systems with Agent-driven R&D.It breaks down recommendation iteration into four agent modules:Brainstorm Agent Translating ambiguous business goals into actionable plans;Developing Agent Writing plans into production code, deploying only after feature validation, DSL checking, and dry-run verification;

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Evaluation Agent Running A/B experiments with safe deployment, traffic splitting, and guardrail vetoes, outputting KEEP / EXTEND / DISCARD;

The most crucial is the fourth:Harness EvolutionDiagnosing each failure as a 'semantic gradient' and using a mechanism called SGPO(Semantic-Gradient-based Prompt Optimization) modifies the agent's own working rules and prompts, validates them against historical experiment replays, and only pushes the new version to production once it proves superior.

Agents are not just tools to help write code; they are evolving into self-improving R&D systems.

Three-week production data: 3 AgentX workers advanced 374 ideas,106 passed plan reviews,100 completed deployment,10 achieved positive evaluations meeting release standards.

Natural funnel convergence:28.34% idea pass rate → 94.3% code-launch rate → 9.9% positive eval rate. Business impact: Main app watch time +0.561%, local services annualized at over 100 million RMB

An even more valuable signal is self-acceleration, with weekly concurrency surging from 15 to 60, idea pass rate at 15%→45%, and weekly releasable results at 2→5. Two rounds of PCV experiments demonstrated the real operation of 'self-iteration': the first round of PCV boosting showed weak positive results lacking significance, while the second round added quality gating and adaptive weights to achieve a +0.071% increase in watch time. Every experiment becomes a better hypothesis for the next.
