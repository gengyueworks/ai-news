07-17 · NVIDIA · Robot Modules

NVIDIA turns Jetson Thor into modular chips for edge LLM inference without cloud dependency, but the real battle is just beginning

NVIDIA releases the Jetson Thor module series (T2000 and T3000), transforming Thor compute originally intended for robotics into pluggable modules.Single-module compute reaches approximately 2000 TOPS, enabling local execution of multimodal large models directly on robot dogs, robotic arms, and factory inspection gear without data center offloading.

Primary intelligence visual archive

Previously, robot 'brains' lived in the cloud and failed during network outages; now NVIDIA aims to embed the compute directly into the chassis while keeping total power consumption at the kilowatt scale.This shifts edge embodied AI from demo videos to production-line components, enabling upgrades via module swaps without rebuilding entire machines.

07-17 · Xiaomi · Robots Enter the Factory

Xiaomi robot internship progress report: screw-driving success rate rises from 90.2% to 98%

After roughly a quarter of factory internship, Xiaomi's robots have improved their bilateral operation success rate at the self-tapping nut assembly station from 90.2% at the start of the year to 98%, coming just one percentage point shy of the 99% accuracy achieved by human workers.They also unlocked two new stations: center console side-cover sorting and bin folding/recycling, both achieving over 90% success rates.

07-17 · Moonshot AI · Open Weights

Kimi K3 Released, 2.8T Parameters: History's Largest Open Model Tops Frontend Coding, Beating Claude Fable 5

Moonshot AIReleased Kimi K3, a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with 2.8 trillion total parameters and 1040 billion active parameters per token,, 896 active experts routed per token out of 16, featuring a 100 million-token context window, native multimodal input, and a commitment to open-source 7 all weights on 27.In terms of parameter scale alone, it is the largest open-weight candidate in history.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

The first stop for topping the leaderboards is coding.Arena In the frontend coding arena, K3 scored 1679 to jump from K2.6's 18 place straight to the top, surpassing Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.This marks the first time an open model has outperformed top closed-source models in human blind-test frontend engineering tasks; pairwise win rate is 76%while Fable 5 is 63%.

It also enters the first tier in comprehensive capabilities. Artificial Analysis gives K3 an intelligence index score of 57, tying with Claude Opus 4.8, though the team admits "a clear gap in user experience remains compared to Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol." Cost is another selling point: input costs 3 per million tokens, output is 15, and cached input gets a 90% discount.

Architecturally, its attention mechanism functions as "memory":Kimi Delta Attention (KDA)the team claims decoding is 6.3 x faster in million-token contexts, and Attention Residuals reportedly improve training efficiency by about 25%. Several engineers immediately compared this release to the "DeepSeek moment for the open-source world."With 2.8 trillion weights promised in ten days and a win over top closed models in frontend engineering, the time gap between open and closed sources has compressed from months to weeks.

Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3 MoE model.

07-17 · Google · Terminal Agent

Google Open-Sources Agents CLI, Run Multi-Step Agents Directly in the Terminal

Google has open-sourced Agents CLI, a tool for orchestrating multi-step agents in the command line: give it a goal, and it breaks down tasks, calls tools, checks results, and determines the next steps, all within the terminal.The project has gained over 5100+ stars in days, targeting developers looking to script agents into pipelines and CI workflows.

It brings agents out of chat windows and into developers' most familiar environment.When agents can be written into a single command like grep, automation truly enters the workflow.
