WAIC 2026 Kicks Off: Hundreds of Vendors Feature "Agents" in Main Slogans, But the Real Hard Battle Has Just Begun

07-18 · State Council · Action Guidelines
AI Action Guidelines Released, 2027 Core Industry Adoption Rate Target at 70%

An action guideline aimed at AI application deployment proposes that by 2027, the application adoption rate of large models in key industries should reach 70%, along with supporting construction targets for three types of foundational infrastructure: computing power, data, and talent.The document places "utilization" before "creation," shifting the focus noticeably toward large-scale deployment.

For the industry, this is a clear demand checklist.When policies turn adoption rates into hard metrics, buyers expand from early adopters to every department that must calculate ROI.

07-18 · Xiaomi · Multi-Machine Collaboration
Xiaomi Robotics Multi-Machine Collaboration, Multiple Robots Synchronize Pace at Tote Box Folding Station

Xiaomi has disclosed new progress on its robots entering the factory floor: at the bin folding and recycling workstation in the logistics area of the general assembly workshop, multiple robots synchronize their operational status and match processing paces through the factory system, achieving stable multi-machine collaboration.Previously, it achieved a dual-sided success rate of 98%.

Moving from "one working robot" to "a coordinated team working in sync" is the final hurdle before mass production. The real difficulty of entering factories lies not in a more flexible individual unit, but in a team that can align with one another.

07-18 · Alibaba · Edge Open Source
Alibaba open-sources new edge model, compact enough to reside locally on phones and vehicle systems

Alibaba open-sources a next-gen edge model focused on "small size, low latency, always-on residency"—compressing model size to several GBs, enabling local residency on mobile phones and car infotainment systems without relying on round-trip cloud responses.Weights and inference examples are released together, targeting offline assistants, in-vehicle voice, and embedded devices.

07-18 · Hugging Face · Benchmark
Hugging Face launches agent benchmark, providing a unified metric for "can it actually do the work"

Hugging Face releases an agent evaluation benchmark that measures task completion rather than just accuracy: given real web pages and tools, it tests whether the agent can independently complete 3 multi-step operations like booking tickets, filling forms, and querying data.It elevates "capability" from the linguistic level to the action level.

Without a benchmark, agents rely solely on demos. Once evaluations can count completed tasks, industry hype space shrinks.

Summary
Summary

Today's keyword is "assigned to work."

On one hand, Stepfun brought native agent phones to WAIC, and the State Council set a 2027 penetration rate target of 70%—moving agents from concepts into products and national agendas.

On the other hand, Xiaomi synced multiple robots to the same rhythm, and hundreds of vendors at WAIC featured "AI Agents" as their primary slogan.

Capital is following the exact same momentum: SambaNova's computing power and hundreds of millions in embodied AI funding are paving the way for "AI that can work."

Brighter models are no longer the only story.

Who can legitimately reside within devices, consistently get things right, and be integrated into real workflows are the three things laid out today.
