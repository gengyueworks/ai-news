07-13 · Research Institution · Long-range Planning Still Abandoned

Benchmark test pours cold water, showing frontier models still abandon tasks midway during long-range planning

For companies planning to deploy agents into critical workflows, this data should be read before any product launch event.

  • High failure rate in long-range planning
  • Frequent silent drifting midway through tasks
  • Read empirical tests before deployment, look beyond demos
