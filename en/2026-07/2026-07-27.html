07-27 · Moonshot AI / Hugging Face · Open Weights

Kimi K3 Full Weights Released, Open Models Reach the 3-Trillion Scale for the First Time

Kimi K3 full weights officially released.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

On July 27, Moonshot AI open-sourced the full weights of Kimi K3 on Hugging Face,2.8 trillion parameters,896 experts activating only 16 per token, and a million-token context window. Under the Modified MIT license, it is available for download, modification, and self-deployment.Additionally, three infrastructural tools were open-sourced: MoonEP, FlashKDA, and AgentEnv.

The leverage held by closed-source labs that "only we can run the frontier" has been loosened by this weight release. The competition moving forward is not about who has a model, but whether you can verify it yourself.

Sources: Moonshot AI · Hugging Face

Microsoft MAI-Cyber-1-Flash achieves a 95.95% offense-defense win rate, 10 points higher than GPT-5.5 Cyber, but the real battle has just begun

Microsoft's security model MAI-Cyber-1-Flash scored 95.95% win rate on CyberGym, surpassing Mythos's 83.8% and GPT-5.5 Cyber's 85.6%; with inference costs at half of benchmarked models, it has been integrated into the MDASH platform, handling about 90% of the workload independently, leaving the remaining 10% to GPT-5.4.

08-03 Preview · Microsoft · Security Evaluation

Project Perception previewing next month, featuring simultaneous Red, Blue, and Green teams

Microsoft announced the Project Perception preview for August 3, splitting model security evaluation into parallel Red, Blue, and Green teams: one attacking, one defending, and one providing independent adjudication, moving beyond single-report assessments. The initial launch will open all three evaluation teams.
