07-19 · WAIC · Embodied AI

Qiji T1000 Ton-Class Robotic Horse: Capable of Carrying One Ton and Entering High-Risk Sites, But the Real Battle Has Just Begun

Featured robots on display at WAIC Qiji T1000, the world's first ton-class heavy-duty quadruped robotic horse, with a maximum payload of 1000 kg, a top speed of 10 km/h, and an endurance of 40 km. Eschewing wheels, it relies on four legs to traverse gravel, mud, and steep slopes.

Two deployment scenarios: high-end off-road riding, and replacing humans in high-risk operations such as cave-ins and landslides.When robots begin taking on primitive heavy labor like "carrying loads," embodied AI truly steps off the demo stage and into the real world.

Sources: 21st Century Business Herald

07-17 · Moonshot AI · Open-Source Model

Kimi K3 Released: 2.8 Trillion Parameters, Top of Frontend Coding Leaderboard, But the Real Battle Has Just Begun

Moonshot AI has released Kimi K3, featuring a parameter scale of 2.8 trillion, making it one of the world's largest open-source models. Its frontend coding arena score surpassed Claude Fable 5. While officially still lagging behind the strongest closed-source models, it has secured a front-row seat in the core programming arena.

Model weights will be made public before July 27.For the first time, an open-source model has outperformed top-tier closed-source models in real-world engineering benchmarks. This isn't a parameter game; it's about long-horizon task execution capability.

Moonshot AI releases the Kimi K3 Mixture-of-Experts model.

Sources: Moonshot AI Open Platform · TMTpost
07-18 · Tencent Cloud · Enterprise Agents

Tencent Cloud ADP 4.0 Overseas Edition: Enterprise Agent Platform Usage Doubled YoY

Tencent Cloud launches its enterprise-grade agent development platform at WAIC ADP4.0 overseas edition, alongside a general-purpose desktop agent. The Vice President stated that platform usage this year has at least doubled 1 YoY.

The bottleneck of enterprise deployment has shifted from model capability to "how to integrate with legacy systems."When agents need to access legacy data in ERP and CRM systems, AI competition shifts from whose model is strongest to who helps enterprises bridge integration gaps first.

Sources: Tencent Cloud
