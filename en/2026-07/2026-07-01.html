Anthropic / TechCrunch · June 30
Anthropic Released Sonnet 5: The cheapest agent model, starting at $2/$10

On paper, Sonnet 5 is indeed cheap: standard pricing at $3/$15 per million tokens, promotional pricing at $2/$10, with a 1M context window. However, third-party evaluations reveal another reality,calculated by the actual cost of completing a task, it may actually be more expensive than Opus 4.8.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Data from Artificial Analysis: Sonnet 5 uses an average of 6.9 ten thousand output tokens per task, about 40% more than Sonnet 4.6, with roughly 3x the number of turns compared to 4.6 in agentic tasks;

translating to an Intelligence Index task cost of $2.29, approximately 1.15 x that of Opus 4.8. simonw points out that the new tokenizer increases English text costs by about 1.4x.

However, capabilities have indeed increased: CursorBench 57%(4.6 was 49%), Cognition tested FrontierCode Extended at 53.8%, surpassing Opus 4.8, and the CritPt physics benchmark gained +14 points. AI Index score is 53, ranking 5th.

Affordability cannot be judged by unit price alone; the true cost per task is becoming the new default evaluation metric.

Anthropic / TechCrunch · June 30
Claude Science Released: The Research Workbench That "Connects the Tools Well"

Anthropic simultaneously released Claude Science yesterday, an AI workstation designed for researchers.Anthropic made it clear:It uses the standard Claude model available to everyone, with no modifications to the underlying architecture.Success or failure does not lie in the model itself, but in whether it is backed by reliable, deterministic tools.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Claude Science can run on a lab's own machines (macOS/Linux), connecting to HPC clusters or Modal cloud resources via SSH. It features built-in 60+ scientific database connectors and specialized modules for genomics, proteomics, and structural biology, all coordinated by a unifying agent.

Every result carries a complete provenance chain—detailing how it was produced, what code and environment were used—enabling replication and verification.

The strategic signal behind this is more noteworthy:Anthropic is replicating the Claude Code playbook: the model is core, but the 'work layer' surrounding it drives true stickiness. For developers it's Claude Code, and for scientists it's Claude Science.

TechCrunch · June 30
AI Employment Data Defies Intuition: High-adoption companies see total headcount +10.2%, junior roles +12%

Ramp and Revelio Labs jointly released an AI employment report based on nearly 22000 companies.The results run counter to popular belief:"Heavy AI adopters" (defined as companies where per capita monthly spend on AI tools over the past 3 months exceeded $30 ) saw overall hiring grow by 10.2%, with junior roles actually growing by 12%, outpacing the average growth.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

This contrasts with another dataset:Goldman Sachs estimates AI has wiped out a net of approximately 16000 jobs per month over the past year. By May 2026, publicly announced AI-related corporate layoffs approached 90000 people.

PwC's Global AI Jobs Barometer also revealed a "two-track market," where AI-"specialized" roles (human expert + AI = stronger) grew at 2 times the rate of AI-"democratized" roles (AI makes anyone capable), with wage growth higher by 42%.

Taken together:AI is neither simply "destroying jobs" nor "creating jobs"; it is redrawing the line on whose work is valuable. Individuals who can leverage AI to amplify their capabilities are heavily contested, while roles stuck in the "replaceable" logic face contraction.

TechCrunch / Bloomberg · June 30
DeepMind Poker AI team pivots to quant, valued at 5 billion

Three former DeepMind researchers—Martin Schmid, Rudolf Kadlec, and Matej Moravcik—created DeepStack in 2017, the first AI to beat humans in no-limit Texas Hold'em.Now they are applying the same reinforcement learning architecture to stock trading.

Primary intelligence field evidence and architecture visual

Their startup, EquiLibre Technologies, just closed a Series A led by Creandum, valued at 5 billion. Partnering with quantitative firm Tower Research Capital, they trade billions daily on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq. The company claims that since launching in crypto markets in 2025,zero losing months, with no monthly losses.

CEO Schmid put it succinctly:"The scoring system for trading and markets is simple: how much money did the agent make?"What poker and markets share: both reward agents for sequential decision-making under incomplete information, and both penalize rigid rules.

OpenAI · June 30
GeneBench-Pro: AI scored lower than you think on its biology final exam

OpenAI has released GeneBench-Pro, a benchmark evaluating AI's "research taste" in computational biology. The tasks are cleverly designed:It gives models a messy real-world dataset, experimental background, and a decision goal to see if they can make research judgments like a human.

Primary intelligence visual archive

Result: The pass rate for the strongest model, GPT-5.6 Sol, is 28.7%. Six months ago, the top GPT-5 was under 5%. Progress is rapid,but we are still far from being able to "trust AI to conduct biology research for you."By open-sourcing this benchmark, OpenAI is tracking "scientific research judgment" as a measurable capability dimension.

  • 129 questions covering 10 fields and 21 subfields
  • Including functional genomics, regulatory genomics, and statistical genetics
  • OpenAI expects this benchmark could be saturated by the end of this year

Daily Summary

First: Models are getting cheaper, Sonnet 5 brings agent entry pricing down to the cost of a cup of coffee. When inference is no longer a bottleneck, the barrier shifts from "can we afford it?" to "do we want to use it?"
