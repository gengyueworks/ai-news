OpenAI Releases 722 Math Manuscripts: Solving Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis with 3 Hours of Compute per Result

OpenAI published a sweeping collection of 722 mathematical manuscripts grouped into 372 result families from an unreleased internal frontier model, developed in consultation with the Institute for Advanced Study's (IAS) advisory group.

The papers stem from an evaluation of approximately 4,000 research-level mathematical problems. Most astonishingly, while September's Navier-Stokes breakthrough required 88 hours and 10,000 collaborative agents, these solutions averaged just three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute per result.

Highlights include Result 003, the Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis, deemed by number theorists the most monumental advance in the discipline in two centuries, alongside the formal elimination of Siegel zeros and resolution of the 3D elastic inverse problem. Crucially, roughly 20% of results constitute disproofs and counterexamples, decisively refuting claims that AI theorem discovery relies solely on brute-force search.

Mistral Launches Trillion-Parameter Large 4: Pre- and Post-Trained on 3,800 European Blackwell GPUs

European AI flagship Mistral AI introduced Large 4, a multimodal model with 1T total and 49B active parameters, now accessible via commercial API.

Trained across a dedicated European cluster of 3,800 Grace Blackwell (GB300) systems, the model's ongoing reinforcement learning run reportedly shows zero signs of performance saturation, setting a new benchmark for sovereign European frontier compute.
