This Time Humans Submitted First: Three Computer Scientists Rush a Unique Games Proof Into Print, Holding the Line for Now

Quanta Magazine documents a rare public race in mathematics.

Amid rumors that an AI system had closed in on the Unique Games Conjecture, three computer scientists hurried to publish their own milestone result.

The conjecture is a pillar of computational complexity theory; its resolution determines the theoretical ceiling of a wide family of approximation algorithms.

Humans submitted first this time, but everyone involved understands that an era of humans and machines working the same problems on the same clock has already begun.

Reflection · Open Foundation Model
Reflection Unveils Beam, a 501B-Parameter Open Model: American Open Source Returns to the Table After a Year

Reflection has released Beam 501B-A23B, a mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active per token, with fully open weights.

The company debuted over a year ago with a focus on coding models, then went quiet long enough for observers to write it off.

Beam directly answers the question of whether the United States can still produce a frontier-class open model, returning open-weight competition to a multipolar race.

The focus of the open ecosystem is shifting from whether models are open at all to whether open foundations can keep pace with the closed frontier.

Perplexity · Embedding Models
Perplexity Releases pplx-embed-v2: A 0.6B Edge Model and a 9B Retrieval Backbone Open-Sourced Together

Perplexity has open-sourced the embedding model pplx-embed-v2-late in two sizes.

The 0.6B variant is built for edge devices and local vectorization; the 9B variant targets high-quality index construction and scores 92.4 on the MADQA benchmark.

Both ship under the MIT license, further handing the core components of retrieval-augmented generation to the community.

The embedding layer is becoming contested open-source territory, because whoever holds the retrieval backbone holds the entrance to RAG applications.
